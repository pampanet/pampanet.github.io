About Us

Pampanet is an independent development studio focused on crafting innovative digital products that merge technology, creativity, and education.

Our work spans mobile and web platforms, using modern frameworks like Flutter, SwiftUI, and WebRTC to deliver smooth, high-performance experiences. We leverage AWS cloud services to scale applications efficiently while maintaining privacy and performance.

Pampanet’s flagship project, El Polímata, represents our mission: to create interactive experiences that entertain, challenge, and inspire curiosity through language, science and discovery.
